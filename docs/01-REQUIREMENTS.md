## 1. Core Concept

ArchFacing is a **B2B + B2C marketplace** that brings every stakeholder in India's
building/design industry onto one platform — architects, interior designers,
consultants, building material suppliers, corporates, students and end clients.

**Two new terms (the platform's identity):**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DESIGNA** | Professionals and products connected with Architecture (design + **A**rchitecture) |
| **DESIGNI** | Professionals and products connected with Interior designing (design + **I**nterior) |

Tagline: *"We the Designa and Designi"*

## 2. PACT — the backbone of the platform

**PACT = PIN Area Code Territory**

An Indian postal PIN code is sold like a franchise territory. This is ArchFacing's
most distinctive feature and its main revenue engine.

### 2.1 The PACT rules (PDF §3.1.8)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Term | 10 years, from the registration date |
| Renewal | recharge at a nominal fee after 10 years |
| Transfer | can be sold to someone with the same qualification, or to family — ArchFacing's approval is required |
| Exclusivity | **one PIN + one service type = one registrant only** (never two) |
| Multiple | one registrant may buy several PACTs / several services (on the same mobile number) |
| Lead flow | after payment, the leads for that PACT go straight into the "My Enquiry" section of their dashboard |

### 2.2 How many seats there are in one PACT (PDF §7.4.2)

| Category | Seats per PACT area |
|---|---|
| Designa (4.1.3) | 1 |
| Designi (4.1.4) | 1 |
| Consultant (4.1.5) | **24 heads** (1 for each consultant sub-category) |
| BMF (4.1.7) | 1 |
| Corporates | not available (will open later) |
| Clients/Visitors | not permitted |

> **This is the single most important business rule** — a seat availability check must
> happen before every PACT purchase.

### 2.3 PACT Cessation (the rules for revocation — §3.1.8.5)

1. Failing to serve clients for 30 days
2. Five complaints arriving from that PACT area
3. Failing to follow ArchFacing's standards
4. All complaints go to the Design Cell, and the PACT holder gets reasonable time

### 3.1 The 4 sub-types of Professional (§3.1.3)

In both Designa and Designi:
1. **Professionals** — practising architects (COA no. required) / interior designers
2. **Legends** — highly experienced, recognised across the industry
3. **Innovators** — recognised for innovation
4. **Students** — **free registration**, can upgrade later

### 3.2 Design Associate (DA) — §3.1.7

DAs are professionals who buy a PACT and choose to work alongside ArchFacing.

**What a DA does:** understand the client's requirement, visit the site, prepare a
design proposal and send it to the Head Design Office, realise payment from the
client, hold meetings 10AM–5PM, and call the Head Office between 2 and 5PM.

**What ArchFacing (AF) does:** examine the design, complete an unfinished design,
raise the invoice against the centralised rate card (on the 1st of every month),
handle taxation, and share material discounts.

**💰 Revenue Split (§3.1.7.7): 80% to the DA, ArchFacing keeps 20%**

**DA benefits:** 10 years of free Club Membership, free or discounted entry to events,
the right to use the ArchFacing logo, and discounted merchandise.

**DA Email convention (§8.2):** `firstname + PACT@gmail.com`
Example: PACT 110005 + Yudhveer → `yudhveer110005@gmail.com`
This "Name + PACT" is also their reference code.

## 7. Registration Flow (§4.4)

```
Mobile → OTP → User Type → Basic Details → Profession Details → choosing a PACT → Payment → Dashboard
```

**Client:** free, just mobile + OTP + basic details
**Student:** free registration, can upgrade later
**Everyone else:** paid + PACT selection + payment

### 7.1 Required fields

**Basic (everyone):** Name, Email, Gender, DOB, Address (PIN=PACT, State), Profile Photo,
Social links (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn)

**Qualification (Professional/Consultant §4.3.3.2):** 10th, 12th, Graduation,
Post-Graduation, PhD (each with Board/University + State + Passing Year),
Professional Membership (Institution, Year, Membership No.), Certificate upload

**Corporate (§4.5.3):** Company Name, GST No., PAN, Logo, Incorporation Date

**Verification:** a **COA number** is required for architects (§3.1.8.1.1),
ArchFacing will verify a Consultant's qualification, and a DA needs **KYC + Tax
documents** (§3.1.7.9)
