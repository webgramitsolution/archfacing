### 2. CONCEPT OF ARCHFACING

Archfacing is a concept of bringing together all the building professionals, consultants, innovators, legends, Products Manufacturers, on a single web platform to make construction and its design developments teams more efficient and more useful to the general public of India. Archfacing will also facilitate the user of this platform as the search facilities to select and find professionals and consultants as per their needs with the help of electronics and modern web technology.

Archfacing is also providing a platform the product manufacturers to showcase and launch their products and projects of their pride.

The unique concept of archfacing is to provide a search engine for professionals and consultant on the basis of PIN code of the area, archfacing is using PIN code of Postal departments as searching tools and use it as delivering address of the professionals, consultants, Building materials Manufactures, traders, and clients.

Archfacing is a web based solution to give spaces on web portal to the young designers to display their school project and creativities. These students are from various fields of art, architecture and interior designing profession.

Archfacing is using first time two new English words namely DESIGNA and DESIGNI, developed by architect Yudhveer Singh, Designa is representing the professionals and products related to the architecture and Designi is representing interior designing and related fields. Thus, Archfacing is all about Designa and Designi which represents in its logo as "We the Designa and Designi". The core words design has been taken into consideration while driving new ones by adding "A" from Architecture as suffix to design called it DESIGNA and "I" from interior designing field, so design become DESIGNI related to Interior designing files.

ArchFacing is using PIN Area Code Territory concept with the help of Modern technology to provide the services and sale of products, franchises to various Processionals, consultants, and Building materials manufactures and traders and end users of any field. I architect Yudhveer Singh is introducing this system of distributing franchise based on PIN Area Code Territory (PACT) system first time in India.
