###### 6.1.2.2.2.1 &nbsp;&nbsp;What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small piece of data that a Portal stores on your device when you visit, typically
containing information about the Portal itself, a unique identifier that allows the portal to
recognise your web browser when you return, additional data that serves the purpose of the
cookie, and the lifespan of the cookie itself.

Cookies are used to enable certain features (e.g. logging in), to track portal usage (e.g.
analytics), to store your user settings (e.g. time zone, notification preferences), and to
personalise your content (e.g. advertising, language).

Cookies set by the Portal you are visiting are normally referred to as "first-party cookies",
and typically only track your activity on that particular portal. Cookies set by other portals
and companies (i.e. third parties) are called "third-party cookies", and can be used to track
you on other Portals that use the same third-party service.

###### 6.1.2.2.2.2 &nbsp;&nbsp;Types of cookies and how we use them

**I. Essential cookies:** Essential cookies are crucial to your experience of a Portal, enabling
core features like user logins, account management, shopping carts and payment processing. We
use essential cookies to enable certain functions on our Portal.

**II. Performance cookies:** Performance cookies are used in the tracking of how you use a
Portal during your visit, without collecting personal information about you. Typically, this
information is anonymous and aggregated with information tracked across all portal users, to
help companies understand visitor usage patterns, identify and diagnose problems or errors their
users may encounter, and make better strategic decisions in improving their audience's overall
Portal experience. These cookies may be set by the Portal you're visiting (first-party) or by
third-party services. We use performance cookies on our portal.

**III. Functionality cookies:** Functionality cookies are used in collecting information about
your device and any settings you may configure on the portal you're visiting (like language and
time zone settings). With this information, Portals can provide you with customised, enhanced or
optimised
